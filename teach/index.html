Teach the assistant

For the FastDoctorBangkok team. Here you write what the AI assistant should know, try it the way a visitor would, run its safety tests and decide what goes live. Signing in lasts 12 hours on this device.

Knowledge

A note goes live only when you publish it. Until then it is a draft: visitors never see it, and you can try it on the right first.

        What comes first

        1. The fixed safety rules, which cannot be changed here: emergency signs always go to emergency care (1669); it never diagnoses, never names a medicine or a dose, never promises a result, quotes only catalog prices, and says it is an AI. Code removes anything that breaks them before a visitor sees it.
        2. What the website says: services, prices, the FAQ, coverage, how it works. Change these on the website.
        3. The medical guide: general health information, awaiting the clinical lead’s review.
        4. Your notes, where one bears on the question.
        5. These instructions: tone and approach.
