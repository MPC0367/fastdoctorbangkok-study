What happens when you ask for help

  1. Tell us what you needYou are here

    Request online, call or message us. Not sure what you need? Ask for a doctor’s assessment.

  2. We review and confirm

    A coordinator checks your location and timing and shows you an estimate. You approve it before we accept.

  3. A named clinician is assigned

    You see who is coming, their role and languages, and when they set off.

  4. Care where you are

    The clinician assesses you. Anything beyond the estimate is priced and agreed with you first.

  5. Documents and follow-up

    You receive a receipt and medical report for insurance, and follow-up as your clinician advises.
