Questions and answers

How much does a visit cost?

A doctor visit starts from ฿4,000. The cost depends on the care you need. You see an estimate before you send a request, and any additional treatment is priced and agreed with you before it is given.

How quickly can someone arrive?

We will arrive at your location within 1 hour, and it might be sooner at night. When your visit is confirmed we give you an estimated arrival time, and you can follow progress in the app.

Are your doctors and nurses qualified?

Visits are carried out by doctors and nurses who hold Thai professional licences. When a clinician is assigned to you, you see their name, role and the languages they speak.

What if I need a hospital?

If the doctor finds you need hospital care or tests that cannot be done where you are, they help arrange a transfer to a suitable hospital. In an emergency, call 1669 or go to the nearest hospital emergency department straight away.

Do your doctors and nurses speak English?

Our team works in English, and a Chinese-speaking doctor may be available. Tell us your preferred language and we show the languages of the clinician assigned to you.

How can I pay?

The team currently accepts cash, Visa, Mastercard, WeChat Pay and Alipay. The payment options available for your visit are shown with your estimate before you confirm, and you always receive an official receipt.

Can I claim from my travel insurance?

After your visit you receive a medical report and an official receipt to submit to your insurer. Whether you are reimbursed depends on your policy, so check with your insurer. We do not promise approval, and we do not bill insurers directly unless we tell you so for your visit.

What can you treat?

We help with common urgent problems that are not life-threatening, such as high fever, food poisoning, dehydration, infections, allergic reactions and minor injuries. For a suspected heart attack or stroke, severe difficulty breathing or heavy bleeding, call 1669 or go to a hospital emergency department immediately.
